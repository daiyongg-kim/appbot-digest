★★★☆☆  Works intermittently
The app is decent, but a few things are annoying to me and my wife. First, the notification comes through (e.g. via Apple Watch or iPhone) that your car is unlocked and offers you the “Lock” button, but then it requires a login to actually lock it. By the time you login the convenience is long gone.

Second and most annoying is that the iPhone key is very unreliable. We also have a Tesla and it never has issues. At least 3-5x per month we hop in the Kia and it won’t recognize the phone key. We can still unlock it remotely, but to start it you have to perform a ridiculous routine that we still haven’t figured out. Usually takes us 5-10 minutes to finally get it started.

Aside from these two issues the app is pretty good. But these are annoying and sometimes very frustrating issues when you can’t start the car. Potentially dangerous too.
